About Seidenberg Scholars

A selective, cohort-based enrichment program for students who want a community centered on computer science, IT, and IS at Pace University.

What the program is

Seidenberg Scholars is a selective, cohort-based enrichment program designed for students who strive for academic excellence and are seeking professional and personal development at the collegiate level. Through specialized courses, workshops, and networking, Seidenberg Scholars shape their Pace Path and strengthen their professional portfolios.

Scholars are expected to participate in the majority of the program's workshops and events, and to act as representatives of the school at events for prospective students.

Who it's for

Students entering the Seidenberg School of Computer Science and Information Systems who are looking for a community-centered CS/IT/IS experience, and who are ready to commit to the cohort as active participants and representatives.

Benefits

  • A $10,000 scholarship, in addition to any other Pace scholarship received — usable toward tuition, housing, meal plans, study abroad, or other costs
  • Access to leadership development and professional development programs
  • Faculty and peer mentoring
  • Collaboration with a cohort of motivated students in technology
  • Advanced placement in summer opportunities and faculty-led research

Requirements

  • Acceptance into the Seidenberg School at Pace University
  • High school GPA of 90 or higher
