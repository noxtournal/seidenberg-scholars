What Scholars are part of

Seidenberg Scholars is a selective, cohort-based enrichment program for students who want a community centered on computer science, IT, and IS at Pace. Through specialized courses, workshops, and networking, Scholars shape their Pace Path and build their professional portfolio alongside a cohort of motivated peers in technology.

Scholars are expected to attend the majority of workshops and events, and to represent the school at events for prospective students.
